Clothing with original artwork, designed in Paris

SixthDrive is a French brand of clothing and accessories. Every piece carries a design created by our team, then it is printed or embroidered to order by partner workshops in Europe and in the United States.

How we work

The same method for every piece, from the drawing to the making.

The design

Every design is created by our team and reviewed before it goes into production. Our designs are original.

Made to order

A piece is printed or embroidered when it is ordered, by a partner workshop in Europe or in the United States. Nothing is produced in advance.

An independent brand

SixthDrive is not affiliated with any video game studio, publisher or distributor, and uses no license from them.

About

SixthDrive was founded in 2026 in Paris. It is the trade name of Maxime BERNAZ, a sole proprietorship registered with the Paris trade and companies register.

A small team creates the designs, prepares the collections and publishes short videos every day, produced in-house, about video game culture. They appear on our own TikTok, Instagram, Threads, YouTube, X and Facebook accounts.

Our internal tool, SixthDrive Atelier

SixthDrive Atelier is our team's internal tool. It publishes our videos to our own accounts at scheduled times, posts a first comment under each one (except on X) and measures their performance. It uses the YouTube API Services, the TikTok for Business API, the Meta APIs and the X API. It is restricted to our team and is authorized only by our own accounts.
